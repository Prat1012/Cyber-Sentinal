Safety & authorization

⚠️ Authorized use only. CyberSentinel must only be used against systems you own or have explicit authorization to assess: localhost, private lab environments, CTF/lab targets and intentionally vulnerable applications. Scanning systems without permission may be illegal.

The platform deliberately excludes destructive exploitation, credential brute-forcing, denial-of-service, malware/persistence and stealth/evasion techniques. By default it refuses to scan public/global IP addresses unless ALLOW_EXTERNAL_TARGETS=true is configured for an authorized external assessment. Directory discovery is rate-limited and bounded to a small wordlist.
